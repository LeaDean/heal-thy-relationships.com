RELATIONSHIP GUIDANCE BY TYPE
=============================

Most emotional charge and life energy flows through our connections with others. Below are practical approaches tailored to the most frequent relationship patterns.

ROMANTIC / INTIMATE PARTNERSHIPS
---------------------------------
These relationships amplify both joy and old emotional patterns. The work is about staying clean and awake together.

- Catch the trigger in the first three seconds. When your partner says something that spikes irritation, name it internally ("This is my old abandonment fear") and take one conscious breath before responding.

- Practice "clean requests" instead of complaints. Translate the emotion into a short, specific ask: "When you leave dishes out I feel resentful — could you put them in the sink right after?" No history or blame.

- Share energy instead of dumping emotions. Take two minutes to breathe and centre before unloading your day's stress. Then share from a grounded place.

- Use physical touch or eye contact as a reset. In tense moments, reach for a hand or make soft eye contact for a few breaths. It bypasses mental stories and reconnects the body-level bond.

- Schedule "presence dates." Once a week, do something ordinary with phones away and full attention on each other. No fixing problems — just being together.

FAMILY RELATIONSHIPS (parents, adult children, siblings)
---------------------------------------------------------
These often carry the deepest automatic patterns. Patience with small, repeated actions is key.

- Meet the role, not the history. When old family dynamics activate, respond to the present person rather than the old story.

- Set gentle boundaries with kindness: "I can't talk about this right now, but I love you." No long explanations. Repeat as needed.

- Give without bookkeeping. Do one small helpful act without expecting thanks or change in return.

- Use gatherings as practice ground. Decide one quality you want to embody before arriving — calm, light-hearted, honest — and return to that quality when emotions rise.

FRIENDSHIPS
-----------
Friendships often fade from unspoken expectations or drifting attention.

- Check your energy investment honestly. Notice which friendships feel mutual and energising versus one-sided or draining.

- Speak truth lightly and soon. If something feels off, bring it up soon in a low-key way before it builds into resentment.

- Celebrate and appreciate out loud. Name what you value in the person during normal conversations.

- Allow natural evolution. When a friendship changes or fades, let it without forcing closure or drama.

WORK RELATIONSHIPS (colleagues, bosses, employees)
---------------------------------------------------
These test your ability to stay present under performance pressure and power dynamics.

- Separate the role from the person. See difficult interactions as role-to-role first ("This is authority energy meeting my old fear of criticism").

- Practice precise feedback. Focus on behaviour and outcomes, not personality.

- Protect your energy field daily. Before meetings, take 30 seconds to feel your own centre and set a quiet intent. After, do a quick energy reclaim.

- Turn gossip or complaining into neutral observation. Redirect to facts or solutions on the spot.

YOUR RELATIONSHIP WITH YOURSELF (the foundation)
-------------------------------------------------
All other relationships improve when this one is tended.

- Treat yourself as you would a respected friend. When you slip up, speak to yourself with the same tone and encouragement you'd offer someone you care about.

- Keep small promises to yourself. Each day honour one commitment to your own well-being.

- Regular self check-ins. Three times a day ask: "How am I feeling right now, and what do I need?" Then give it if possible.

In sessions we usually map which relationship type is currently costing you the most energy and pick two or three micro-practices to test for the following week. These approaches aren't about changing other people — they're about changing how you show up, which naturally shifts the dynamic.
