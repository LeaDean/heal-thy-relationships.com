My Approach in a Nutshell
--------------------------
I work with clients who want simple, grounded ways to bring more clarity, freedom, and ease into everyday life — especially around emotions and relationships. My approach blends ancient contemplative traditions with contemporary neuroscience and emotional intelligence frameworks.

Everything we do together is practical. No retreats required. No special tools. Just small, repeatable steps you can weave into your normal routine.
