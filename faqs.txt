FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
===========================

Q: What actually happens in a session?
A: We start exactly where you are. You don't need to have it all figured out or know what to say. I'll ask a few gentle questions to understand what's feeling most alive or stuck for you right now. From there we work practically — looking at specific situations in your life, identifying patterns, and finding small concrete steps you can test immediately. You'll leave each session with something real to work with, not just insight.

Q: Is this therapy?
A: No. This is guidance and coaching, not therapy or counselling. We focus on practical tools for everyday life — emotional awareness, relationship skills, and small actions that create real change. If someone needs clinical mental health support, I'll always say so honestly and help point them in the right direction.

Q: I've never done anything like this before. Is that okay?
A: Absolutely. Many clients come with no background in coaching or personal development at all. All that's needed is a willingness to be honest and a genuine desire for things to feel different. The work meets you exactly where you are.

Q: How many sessions will I need?
A: This varies for everyone. Many clients notice a real shift within just a few sessions. Others prefer to work consistently over a longer period. We never lock you into a package — you can go session by session and decide as you go.

Q: Do you work online?
A: Yes. Online sessions work just as well as in person. All you need is a quiet space and a reliable internet connection. Sessions are available in person in Guildford too.

Q: What if I'm not sure this is right for me?
A: That's exactly what the free 20-minute discovery call is for. It's a relaxed, no-pressure conversation with absolutely no obligation. You just get to talk and see how it feels.

Q: I feel quite overwhelmed right now. Can you still help?
A: Yes. Feeling overwhelmed is one of the most common reasons people reach out. We start slowly and gently, and the practical tools we use are specifically designed to help with exactly that feeling.

Q: What if things come up that feel too big or too painful?
A: Your safety and wellbeing always come first. If something arises that is beyond the scope of coaching — such as trauma that needs clinical support — I will tell you honestly and help you find appropriate professional help. We never push further than feels right.

Q: Is everything I share confidential?
A: Yes. Everything discussed in sessions is completely confidential. Your privacy is taken seriously.

Q: How do I get started?
A: Book a free 20-minute discovery call. It costs nothing and there's no obligation. Just reach out and we'll find a time to talk.
